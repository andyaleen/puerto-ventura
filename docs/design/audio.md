# Audio & Music

Audio in **Puerto Ventura** should reinforce the feeling of living on a vibrant tropical island without demanding the player's attention. The soundtrack, ambient sounds, and sound effects should create a relaxing atmosphere that naturally evolves as the island is restored. Like the visuals, the audio should tell the story of Puerto Ventura coming back to life.

For the prototype, AI-generated music and sound effects are an excellent starting point. They allow rapid iteration before investing in a custom soundtrack or professional sound design later in development.

# Audio Philosophy

The audio experience should feel warm, calming, and immersive.

Every region should have its own identity, but all should share a cohesive tropical musical style. Players should subconsciously notice the island becoming livelier as restoration progresses, with fuller music, richer ambience, and more environmental activity.

Major gameplay moments—such as completing a restoration, discovering a new region, or catching a legendary fish—should receive memorable audio cues that make accomplishments feel rewarding.

# Ambient Audio

Ambient sounds provide the foundation of the game's atmosphere.

Each region should feature its own looping soundscape, for example:

- **Beach:** Ocean waves, seagulls, palm trees swaying, distant boats.
- **Harbor:** Dock creaks, gulls, fishermen, gentle water movement.
- **City Center:** Fountain water, footsteps, conversations, birds.
- **Jungle:** Insects, birds, waterfalls, rustling leaves.
- **Desert:** Wind, shifting sand, distant echoes.
- **Rocky Mining Area:** Pickaxes, dripping water, distant cave ambience.
- **Volcano:** Bubbling lava, steam vents, low volcanic rumbles.
- **Mountain:** Wind through trees, waterfalls, wildlife.
- **Cursed Area:** Eerie wind, whispers, distant magical sounds.

These loops should be subtle enough that players can spend long sessions in one area without fatigue.

# Music

Puerto Ventura's soundtrack should evolve alongside the island.

Early in the game, music is simple and understated, reflecting the island's neglected state. As districts are restored, additional instruments, harmonies, and melodies are introduced, making familiar themes feel warmer and more hopeful.

Each major region should have its own musical identity while sharing common motifs that unify the island.

Major story moments, festivals, and emotional scenes should receive unique themes that players associate with those milestones.

# Sound Effects

Moment-to-moment gameplay relies on clear, satisfying sound feedback.

Core sound effects include:

- Footsteps
- Resource gathering
- Item pickups
- Menu interactions
- Dialogue advancement
- Building interactions
- Crafting
- Tool usage
- Fishing casts and splashes
- Restoration construction

Every interaction should feel responsive without becoming repetitive.

# Audio Stingers

Short musical stingers celebrate important accomplishments.

Examples include:

- Restoration completed
- Region unlocked
- Rare fish caught
- New recipe learned
- Relationship level increased
- Major story discovery
- Festival beginning

These moments should feel rewarding while remaining gentle enough to match the game's cozy atmosphere.

# Design Principles

The audio system should follow several core principles:

- Favor relaxing, high-quality looping ambience over constant musical intensity.
- Regional soundscapes should reinforce each area's unique identity.
- Restoration should be audible as well as visible, with towns sounding busier as they recover.
- UI interactions should provide clear, consistent feedback.
- Combat music should remain restrained and never overpower the game's overall atmosphere.
- Default volume levels should be comfortable for extended play sessions.

# Implementation Status

Audio has not yet been implemented in the current prototype.

The initial development plan is to use AI-generated music, ambient loops, and sound effects to rapidly prototype the game's atmosphere. As the project matures, these assets can gradually be replaced with custom compositions and professionally designed audio where appropriate.

# Open Questions

Several audio decisions will be explored during development:

- Should the final game use a fully custom soundtrack or begin with CC0/royalty-free assets?
- Should each region have its own independent musical theme, or should one central island theme evolve through different arrangements?
- Can dynamic music transition smoothly as districts are restored and become more vibrant?
- How much procedural ambient audio should react to weather, time of day, and player progression?

The long-term goal is for players to recognize Puerto Ventura not just by its visuals, but by its sound—a peaceful tropical world whose music and ambience become richer as the island comes back to life.
