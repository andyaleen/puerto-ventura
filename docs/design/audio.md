# Audio

## Goals

Support cozy tone and regional identity. Soft ambient beds, clear UI/interact feedback, musical cues when the island “wakes up” (restorations, festivals).

## Likely layers

| Layer | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| Ambience | Waves on Beach, jungle insects, desert wind, harbor gulls |
| Music | Calm day theme; warmer motifs as districts restore |
| SFX | Footsteps, pickup, interact, fade whoosh, fishing splash |
| Stingers | Restoration complete, region unlock, rare catch |

## Design rules

- Prefer looping ambiances that don’t fatigue
- Restoration completion should be audible as well as visible
- Keep volume defaults comfortable; no harsh combat stingers unless combat is confirmed

Not started. No audio pipeline documented in the prototype yet.
