# Locations

Puerto Ventura is divided into a series of interconnected regions, each with its own identity, gameplay systems, characters, resources, and restoration opportunities. Rather than functioning as isolated levels, every location contributes to the larger feeling of exploring and rebuilding a living island. Players transition between regions through natural exits, with each area loading seamlessly as they travel, creating the sense of one large connected world.

The island is intentionally designed to encourage curiosity. Every region contains hidden paths, shortcuts, secrets, restoration projects, and reasons to revisit as the player's tools and abilities improve. Many areas appear inaccessible early in the game, but gradually open through exploration, rebuilding infrastructure, and story progression.

# Early Game Locations

## Beach

The Beach is the player's introduction to Puerto Ventura after surviving the plane crash. It features white sand beaches, rocky coves, scattered shipwreck debris, and the player's first encounters with gathering and fishing. The nearby lighthouse serves as an early landmark, while simple beach resources such as shells, driftwood, coconuts, and fish introduce the game's gathering systems. As restoration progresses, the beach becomes livelier with visitors, decorations, and improved facilities.

## Farm / Home

Just north of the Beach lies the player's home and small farming plot. This serves as the player's permanent base throughout the game, offering space for crops, storage, workshops, crafting stations, and eventually livestock. The homestead expands gradually as the player progresses, remaining cozy rather than becoming an industrial-scale farm. customizable to be a scenic home, rather than only focused on money making.

## Beach Harbor

The Harbor is the island's primary coastal district and one of the first major restoration projects. At the start of the game it is largely abandoned, with damaged docks, boarded-up buildings, and only a handful of struggling businesses remaining. As players invest resources into rebuilding, the harbor becomes home to fishing boats, cafés, seafood markets, and merchants, eventually reconnecting Puerto Ventura to the outside world through trade and tourism.

## Idyllic City Center

The City Center represents the cultural heart of Puerto Ventura. Although worn down when the player first arrives, it retains hints of the island's former beauty through elegant architecture, plazas, fountains, gardens, and public buildings. As restoration continues, it becomes the busiest area on the island and hosts festivals, markets, community events, and important story moments.

## Jungle

The Jungle is the player's first major exploration zone. Dense vegetation, ancient ruins, waterfalls, hidden shrines, and abundant wildlife make it feel alive and mysterious without being overwhelmingly dangerous. Exploration focuses on gathering resources, solving environmental puzzles, clearing blocked paths, and discovering forgotten structures rather than heavy combat.

Players gradually unlock new sections of the jungle by cutting vines, repairing bridges, and upgrading their tools.

# Mid & Late Game Locations

## Deep Jungle

Far beyond the familiar jungle lies the Deep Jungle, a much older and more mysterious ecosystem. Ancient temples disappear beneath the canopy, rare resources become common, and environmental puzzles become more elaborate. Wildlife grows more dangerous, while the hidden jungle tribe and many of the island's oldest secrets begin to emerge.

## Volcano

Located in the island's northwest corner, the Volcano dominates Puerto Ventura's skyline. Initially inaccessible, it becomes reachable only after significant jungle restoration opens the surrounding pathways. The lower slopes contain surprisingly fertile volcanic soil suitable for a unique farming area, while higher elevations introduce lava fields, volcanic caves, geothermal vents, and ancient ceremonial sites watched over by the Volcano Shaman.

## Desert

The Desert was once a flourishing valley before its ancient irrigation systems failed. Early in the game it is dry, barren, and difficult to traverse. As players restore aqueducts and water channels, portions of the desert gradually transform into a vibrant oasis with new wildlife, vegetation, farming opportunities, and settlements, making it one of the island's most visually dramatic transformations.

## Desert Cave

Hidden beneath the desert lies a vast cave network filled with crystals, abandoned machinery, forgotten workshops, and clues about the island's ancient civilization. This area expands the mining system while introducing puzzles, environmental hazards, and deeper layers of the island's technological mystery.

## Rocky Mining Area

The Rocky Mining Area serves as Puerto Ventura's primary source of ore and minerals. Abandoned mine shafts, processing facilities, and excavation sites provide the resources necessary for advanced tool upgrades and crafting. As players restore operations, the mines become safer, more productive, and home to new NPCs and businesses supporting the island's economy.

## Scary Cursed Part

The island's southeastern corner is shrouded in darkness and superstition. Twisted forests, abandoned buildings, strange magical phenomena, and eerie ruins create a dramatically different atmosphere from the rest of Puerto Ventura. This late-game area introduces magic, teleportation mechanics, supernatural lore, and one of the game's deeper storylines, while remaining balanced with the game's overall sense of humor and adventure.

## Secret Ancient High Tech Town

One of Puerto Ventura's greatest mysteries lies hidden beyond ordinary exploration. High above the desert, concealed behind ancient defenses, is a remarkably advanced settlement that changes everything the player believes about the island's history. Its architecture, technology, and inhabitants reveal that Puerto Ventura's ancient civilization was far more sophisticated than anyone imagined, becoming one of the game's defining discoveries 2/3 of the way through. After reaching the high tech town, it initially disappears, until being reopened via questline later. If a player returns before reopening it, it blocks the player saying "You are experiencing a severe headache."

## Mountain

The Mountain region provides a quieter contrast to the island's harsher environments. Snow, waterfalls, hiking trails, cabins, and an abandoned observatory make it one of the most scenic locations in the game. With no combat, the Mountain emphasizes exploration, gathering, peaceful NPC interactions, and uncovering the island's natural beauty.

## Mountain Summit

Accessible only near the end of the game, the Mountain Summit overlooks the entire island. Home to a secluded community of shamans living above the clouds, this region serves as the setting for major story revelations, philosophical conversations, and the culmination of many of Puerto Ventura's central mysteries. Reaching the summit represents one of the player's greatest accomplishments and symbolizes just how far both they and the island have come.
