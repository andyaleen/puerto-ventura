# Story

Puerto Ventura begins not with a chosen hero, but with an accident.

The player is a passenger aboard a small regional aircraft flying over the ocean when severe weather forces an emergency. After losing power, the plane crashes just offshore of Puerto Ventura. The player survives, washes ashore unconscious, and awakens on the island's beach with no supplies and no immediate way home.

A local fisherman discovers the player and helps them regain consciousness. Rather than reacting with panic, he calmly explains that although Puerto Ventura isn't uncharted or forgotten, it has been slowly dying for decades. Ferry routes have stopped, infrastructure has collapsed, communication equipment barely functions, and very few ships visit anymore. Leaving the island is theoretically possible—but not anytime soon.

With nowhere else to stay, the player is offered an abandoned farmhouse overlooking the beach. The fisherman jokes that if you're going to be stranded, you might as well earn your keep. He suggests helping the struggling town while everyone figures out how to contact the mainland.

The player quickly learns that survival isn't difficult, but rebuilding will be.

Food can be gathered from the beaches, fish can be caught from the docks, and basic materials can be collected from the surrounding forests. These simple activities introduce the game's core systems while providing enough money and resources to begin the island's first restoration projects.

The earliest objective is straightforward: help Puerto Ventura function again.

---

# Act I — Rebuilding Civilization

The first major focus is restoring the island's basic infrastructure.

The harbor has fallen into disrepair, the lighthouse barely operates, the fish market is abandoned, and the general store struggles to remain open. Working alongside the island's remaining residents, the player repairs docks, rebuilds public spaces, restores fishing operations, and gradually brings businesses back to life.

Each completed project visibly transforms the town.

Fishing boats return.

Visitors begin arriving.

Buildings reopen.

Music becomes livelier.

The island slowly begins to feel hopeful again.

As prosperity returns, more former residents decide to come home. New merchants establish businesses, families reopen abandoned houses, and the once-empty streets gradually become populated with life.

The player is no longer simply surviving—they are becoming an important part of Puerto Ventura's future.

---

# The Rocky Mining Area

Rebuilding soon reaches a bottleneck.

The island desperately needs stronger construction materials, but the old mines have collapsed after years of neglect. The entrance is blocked, tunnels have caved in, and broken supports make deeper excavation impossible.

After restoring access to the Rocky Mining Area, the player begins gathering stone, iron, copper, coal, and other valuable resources. Better mining equipment allows increasingly ambitious restoration projects while unlocking improved tools and crafting stations.

Among the discoveries are strange mechanical components buried deep within the oldest sections of the mine.

No one remembers who built them.

---

# Reconnecting the Island

Using newly acquired metals and components, local engineers begin rebuilding Puerto Ventura's communications network.

Old radio towers, electrical systems, and long-range communication equipment slowly return to operation.

Eventually, contact with the outside world is restored.

Cargo ships begin visiting again.

New merchants arrive.

Additional businesses reopen around the harbor.

Tourists begin hearing rumors about the island's remarkable recovery.

At this point, the player is offered an opportunity.

A merchant captain explains that there is now enough transportation to leave Puerto Ventura and return home.

The player can choose to board the departing boat.

Doing so immediately ends the game, showing that the player escaped and returned to ordinary life. After the credits, they may reload their save before making the decision.

The intended choice, however, is to stay.

By now, Puerto Ventura has become more than a temporary refuge—it has become home.

---

# Act II — Exploring the Island

With the town thriving again, attention shifts inland.

The jungle, long ignored by the townspeople, begins revealing ancient ruins, forgotten shrines, and overgrown pathways. Players discover new farming opportunities, rare plants, hidden temples, and increasing evidence that Puerto Ventura was once home to a civilization far more advanced than anyone imagined.

News of these archaeological discoveries spreads beyond the island.

Researchers.

Historians.

Collectors.

Adventurers.

Scientists.

They begin arriving to study the ruins, adding new characters, businesses, and quests to the growing community.

As exploration expands, players obtain climbing equipment and grappling gear that allow access to the island's eastern desert.

---

# The Desert

The desert appears lifeless at first.

Its abandoned settlement sits half-buried beneath sand, its irrigation canals dry after centuries of neglect.

By restoring ancient aqueducts and redirecting water, players gradually transform the barren landscape into a flourishing oasis. Vegetation returns, wildlife reappears, and a forgotten desert community slowly comes back to life.

Deep beneath the dunes lies the Desert Cave.

Here, players discover crystalline formations, advanced machinery, and increasingly impossible technology that seems far older than it should exist.

The mystery surrounding Puerto Ventura deepens considerably.

---

# The Deep Jungle

The deepest parts of the jungle remain inaccessible until players obtain an animal (horse or camel) and resources to cross a large expanse.

Within the deeper jungle rainforest lives an isolated indigenous tribe that has spent generations protecting sacred locations while avoiding the outside world.

Through friendship rather than conquest, the player gradually earns their trust.

The tribe shares fragments of ancient history that contradict everything the island's modern residents believe.

Legends speak not of magic...

...but of forgotten technology so advanced it became indistinguishable from magic.

---

# Act III — Fire, Darkness, and Forgotten Power

After restoring access through the jungle, players construct a bridge leading toward the Volcano.

Although dangerous, its fertile volcanic soil creates the island's richest farmland, introducing unique crops unavailable elsewhere.

The volcano also contains Puerto Ventura's first true boss encounter—a powerful guardian protecting an ancient ceremonial site.

Upon reaching the summit, the player discovers an ancient Ring of Power, an artifact that grants a permanent gameplay ability and hints that other relics remain hidden across the island.

Below the volcano lies another remarkable discovery.

An abandoned geothermal power facility.

Although dormant, restoring it provides enough energy to reactivate long-forgotten systems hidden throughout Puerto Ventura.

Meanwhile, unsettling rumors spread through town.

Residents whisper about strange, green and purple lights.

Missing travelers.

Voices heard at night. Creatures in the night sky.

The southeastern corner of the island—known simply as the Scary Cursed Part—has long been avoided by everyone.

When the player finally enters, the atmosphere changes dramatically.

Dark forests replace tropical beaches.

Ancient corruption infects the landscape.

Light combat becomes more frequent.

Strange magical materials begin appearing.

Deep within the cursed wilderness stands the castle of an enigmatic witch.

The player finds it...

...but cannot yet enter.

Whatever seals the castle appears connected to the island's oldest secrets.

---

# Act IV — The Truth About Puerto Ventura

Near the far edge of the restored desert, the player unexpectedly discovers an impossible city.

Perfect towers.

Glowing structures.

Technology centuries beyond modern civilization.

Before they can enter, the city suddenly vanishes.

Later investigation reveals that it has been concealed beneath an ancient energy shield powered by technology hidden elsewhere on the island.

Only after restoring the geothermal facility can enough power be generated to disable the shielding system.

Beyond the barrier lies the Secret Ancient High-Tech Town.

Its surviving inhabitants reveal that Puerto Ventura was once the center of an extraordinarily advanced civilization. Following a catastrophic event generations ago, they isolated themselves from the outside world, hiding both their city and their technology to prevent history from repeating itself.

Everything the player has restored has unknowingly been rebuilding pieces of their forgotten infrastructure.

---

# Act V — Above the Clouds

At the conclusion of the island's first year, spring runoff melts the mountain snowpack, creating waterfalls that shatter ancient rockfalls blocking the northern trail.

Even then, players still require upgraded tools—or carefully placed explosives—to clear the final boulders.

Beyond them lies the Mountain.

Peaceful forests, waterfalls, an abandoned observatory, a tibetan inspired restaurant and quiet cabins provide a calm contrast to the mysteries below.

The final ascent requires advanced climbing equipment.

At the summit, above the clouds, lives an isolated Shangri-La-like community whose wise llama-like guardians (or another mystical species if the design evolves) have watched over Puerto Ventura for centuries.

They reveal the island's final truths.

Not every mystery needs solving.

Not every technology should be rediscovered.

And sometimes, rebuilding a community matters more than uncovering every secret.

By the end of the journey, the player realizes they were never truly trying to find a way home.

Home was the island they helped rebuild.
