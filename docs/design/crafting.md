# Crafting System

Crafting in **Puerto Ventura** is designed to support the core gameplay pillars of exploration, restoration, and progression. Rather than becoming a complex factory simulation, crafting should feel intuitive, rewarding, and purposeful. Every recipe should answer the question: **"What does this help me accomplish?"**

The player crafts better tools to reach new places, processes raw materials into useful building supplies, creates equipment for expeditions, and manufactures the components needed to restore Puerto Ventura. Crafting should never feel like busywork—it exists to help the player rebuild the island.

# Crafting Philosophy

Crafting should remain approachable throughout the game.

The goal is not to overwhelm players with hundreds of recipes or long production chains. Instead, players gradually unlock a curated collection of meaningful recipes that expand naturally as new regions, resources, and technologies are discovered.

Every new crafting station or recipe should immediately unlock new gameplay opportunities rather than simply adding another layer of complexity.

If a crafting system doesn't help the player explore further, restore the island, or improve their homestead, it likely doesn't belong in the game.

# Tool Crafting & Upgrades

The player's tools represent one of the most important forms of progression.

Improved equipment allows players to gather resources more efficiently while also granting access to previously unreachable parts of the island.

Primary tools include:

- Hoe
- Watering Can
- Fishing Rod
- Pickaxe
- Axe
- Shovel
- Scythe
- grappling/climbing hook

Tools can be upgraded through multiple tiers using increasingly rare materials discovered throughout the island.

Rather than simply increasing speed, upgrades should unlock entirely new interactions.

Examples include:

- Stronger pickaxes breaking harder rock formations.
- Better axes clearing massive jungle trees.
- Improved fishing rods reaching deeper waters.
- Advanced shovels uncovering buried ruins.
- Higher-quality tools harvesting rare resources without damaging them.
- climbing hook gets over cliffs

Each upgrade should make the player excited to revisit older areas with new capabilities.

# Crafting Stations

As Puerto Ventura grows, players unlock specialized crafting stations that expand what can be produced.

Potential stations include:

- Workbench
- Smelter
- Sawmill
- Kiln
- Loom
- Kitchen
- Chemistry Set
- Jewelry Bench
- Enchanting Table (late game)
- Restoration Workshop

Each station introduces a focused set of recipes rather than becoming a massive all-purpose crafting menu.

This keeps progression understandable while making each new workshop feel meaningful.

# Resource Processing

Many materials gathered throughout the island are only useful after refinement.

Examples include:

Wood → Lumber

Stone → Cut Stone

Ore → Metal Bars

Clay → Bricks

Sand → Glass

Fiber → Rope

Coral → Decorative Materials

Flowers → decoration and gifts

Herbs → Medicines

These processed materials become the foundation for building upgrades, crafting projects, and island restoration.

The emphasis should be on simple, logical conversions rather than lengthy production chains.

# Restoration Materials

One of crafting's primary purposes is producing the materials needed to rebuild Puerto Ventura.

Instead of delivering raw logs and stone directly to construction projects, players refine resources into higher-quality building supplies.

Examples include:

- Lumber
- Stone Blocks
- Roof Tiles
- Steel Beams
- Glass Panels
- Concrete
- Decorative Masonry
- Plumbing Components
- Electrical Components (late game)
- Historic Restoration Materials

These materials are used to repair homes, bridges, docks, museums, marketplaces, irrigation systems, and public spaces throughout the island.

This creates a satisfying loop where exploration provides resources, crafting transforms them into useful materials, and restoration visibly changes the world.

# Equipment & Expedition Supplies

Crafting also prepares players for exploration.

Useful items may include:

- Torches
- Rope
- Campfires
- Repair Kits
- Healing Supplies
- Food
- Fishing Bait
- Bombs for mining
- Climbing Equipment
- Diving Gear

These items support adventure without requiring excessive inventory management.

Players should feel prepared for an expedition rather than burdened by logistics.

# Recipe Progression

Recipes are earned naturally through exploration and gameplay.

Players unlock new recipes by:

- Upgrading crafting stations
- Building friendships
- Restoring buildings
- Discovering ancient blueprints
- Reading books
- Completing quests
- Exploring hidden ruins
- Learning from specialized NPCs

New recipes should feel like meaningful rewards rather than simply increasing the size of a crafting menu.

# Design Principles

The crafting system should follow several guiding principles throughout development:

- Prefer a small number of clear, memorable recipes over hundreds of nearly identical ones.
- Unlock recipes gradually as the player progresses through the island.
- Crafting should primarily support exploration and restoration rather than becoming a game unto itself.
- Resource conversions should be intuitive and easy to understand.
- Avoid deep factory automation or overly complex production chains.
- Keep menus clean, readable, and easy to navigate.
- Every crafted item should have a clear purpose and immediate value.

# The Crafting Loop

The crafting loop is intentionally straightforward:

**Explore** new regions and gather raw materials.

↓

**Process** those materials into useful building supplies, equipment, and tools.

↓

**Craft** upgraded gear, restoration materials, and expedition supplies.

↓

**Restore** buildings, unlock new areas, and improve your homestead.

↓

**Gain access** to new resources, recipes, and technologies.

↓

**Repeat**, with each cycle expanding the player's capabilities and bringing Puerto Ventura one step closer to becoming a thriving paradise.

Crafting should never distract from the heart of the game. Instead, it should quietly support every major system, giving players the tools they need to explore farther, restore more of the island, and steadily transform Puerto Ventura from a neglected tropical outpost into a vibrant, living community.
