# Fishing System

Fishing is one of the core pillars of Puerto Ventura and serves as one of the player's early-game source of income, not so important later in the game. More than simply earning money, fishing encourages exploration, rewards restoration, and gives players another reason to revisit familiar locations as the island evolves.

The waters surrounding Puerto Ventura are as diverse as the island itself. From quiet beaches and rocky coves to jungle rivers, mountain lakes, hidden caves, and offshore reefs, every body of water offers unique species to discover. As players restore the island's ecosystems, fish populations recover, opening opportunities to catch species that were previously absent from neglected waters.

# Fishing Philosophy

Fishing should feel peaceful, satisfying, and rewarding.

The goal is not to create an overly difficult reflex game, but rather a relaxing activity that fits naturally into the daily rhythm of Puerto Ventura. Whether players spend ten minutes catching dinner or dedicate an entire day to filling their tackle box, fishing should always feel like time well spent.

Like every major system in the game, fishing supports exploration and restoration rather than existing independently.

# Fishing Progression

Players begin with a simple fishing rod capable of catching common coastal fish from the beach near their home.

As better equipment is crafted and restored infrastructure becomes available, entirely new fishing opportunities open across the island.

Potential fishing locations include:

- Beach shoreline
- Wooden docks
- Harbor
- Jungle rivers
- Hidden waterfalls
- Desert oasis
- Mountain lakes
- Underground cave pools
- Offshore reefs
- Secret late-game fishing locations

Upgraded fishing rods allow players to cast farther, reach deeper water, and catch increasingly valuable fish.

# Fishing Gameplay

The core fishing loop is intentionally straightforward.

The player:

- Travels to a fishing location.
- Casts their line.
- Waits for a bite.
- Completes a simple timing or reaction-based minigame.
- Lands the fish and adds it to their inventory.

The exact minigame will be refined during development, but the guiding principle is clear:

**Fishing should be cozy, relaxing, and satisfying—not frustrating or punishing.**

Success should rely more on preparation, upgraded equipment, and choosing the right location than on demanding mechanical skill.

# Uses for Fish

Caught fish have many purposes throughout the game.

Players can:

- Sell them for income
- Cook meals
- Give them as gifts
- Complete NPC requests
- Supply restaurants
- Donate rare specimens
- Use certain species in restoration projects
- Export premium catches for additional profit

Fishing should remain relevant throughout the entire game instead of becoming obsolete once farming expands.

# Fishing & Restoration

Fishing plays an important role in rebuilding Puerto Ventura.

Early in the game, damaged docks and abandoned piers limit where players can fish.

As restoration progresses:

- Docks are rebuilt.
- The harbor reopens.
- Fishing boats return.
- New piers become accessible.
- Cleaner waterways support healthier ecosystems.
- Previously rare fish begin appearing.

The player doesn't simply unlock better fishing spots—they actively restore them.

Watching empty docks become bustling fishing communities reinforces the core theme of bringing the island back to life.

# The Harbor Economy

One of the earliest major restoration projects is rebuilding the harbor and reopening the island's fish market.

Once operational, the harbor introduces:

- Better fish prices
- Specialty bait
- Improved fishing equipment
- Fishing-related quests
- Local fishing competitions

Later, the opening of Puerto Ventura's fine dining restaurant creates demand for premium seafood, encouraging players to pursue higher-quality catches.

In the late game, restored shipping routes allow valuable seafood to be exported beyond the island, creating another meaningful source of income and demonstrating Puerto Ventura's return as a thriving trading port.

# Rare Fish & Collection Goals

Not every fish exists to be sold.

Hidden throughout the island are rare and legendary species that reward exploration and progression.

Some fish only appear:

- In newly restored ecosystems
- At hidden locations
- During certain weather conditions
- At specific times of day
- In secret late-game areas

Collecting these rare species provides optional long-term goals for completionists while encouraging players to revisit earlier regions after restoration projects are complete.

# Design Principles

The fishing system should follow several core principles:

- Fishing should remain one of the strongest early-game income sources.
- The minigame should be relaxing and accessible rather than mechanically demanding.
- Better rods should unlock new opportunities, not simply increase catch speed.
- Different regions should provide distinct fish species that encourage exploration.
- Restoring the island should directly improve fishing opportunities and fish populations.
- Fishing should remain valuable throughout the entire game by supporting cooking, gifting, quests, restaurants, and exports.

# The Fishing Loop

The fishing gameplay loop is simple and rewarding.

**Explore** the island to discover new fishing locations.

↓

**Catch** fish using progressively better equipment.

↓

**Sell, cook, gift, donate, or export** your catch.

↓

**Restore** docks, harbors, and waterways to unlock new species and better fishing opportunities.

↓

**Return** to newly restored waters to discover what has changed.

Fishing should always reinforce the central fantasy of Puerto Ventura: every cast contributes, in some small way, to bringing life back to the island.
