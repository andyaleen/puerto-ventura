# Fishing

## Role

**Major pillar.** Should feel as important as gathering for daily play, especially at Beach and Harbor docks.

## Fantasy

Cast from piers, beaches, and later special waters. Catch variety reflects time, place, and island recovery (cleaner restored waters may yield different fish).

## Loop sketch

1. Reach a fishing spot (Beach dock / Harbor)
2. Use rod (tool upgrade gates better catches)
3. Mini-loop / timing challenge (TBD—keep cozy, not punishing)
4. Sell, cook, gift, or turn in for restorations / NPC needs

## Ties to restoration

- Fish shop restoration in Harbor
- Dock repairs enable better spots
- Rare fish as soft goals after areas reopen

## Implementation status

Design-only. Dock landmarks exist on Beach map intent; no fishing gameplay yet.

## Priority

Build after the first restoration project is wired end-to-end.
