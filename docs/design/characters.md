# Characters & Residents

The people of Puerto Ventura are just as important as the island itself. Every resident should feel like they have their own goals, routines, relationships, and reasons for staying—or returning—to the island. Rather than existing solely to serve the player, the townspeople are rebuilding Puerto Ventura alongside them.

The cast should strike a balance between grounded and eccentric. Most characters are ordinary people with believable motivations, but many have quirky personalities, humorous dialogue, or unusual life stories. The tone should remain warm and playful, allowing even the stranger residents to feel charming rather than absurd.

Unlike many life simulators where the town population is largely static, Puerto Ventura's cast expands throughout the game. As new districts are restored, new residents move in, old friends return home, and hidden groups of people are discovered in previously inaccessible areas. Watching the community grow is one of the game's primary rewards.

# The Player
The protagonist is intentionally left mostly undefined. Like Stardew Valley, the player character speaks very little (or not at all), allowing players to project themselves into the adventure. Their personality is expressed through actions rather than dialogue. The game begins when their plane crashes near Puerto Ventura during a tropical storm. After washing ashore with almost nothing, they gradually become the driving force behind rebuilding the island.

Fisherman / Boat Captain – A gruff but kind-hearted island veteran who knows every reef, current, and fishing spot. He teaches fishing, eventually offers boat transportation, and unlocks offshore exploration.

Farmer – One of the few remaining residents, keeping the island fed despite limited resources. Provides farming guidance, seeds, and agricultural upgrades as the town grows.

General Store Owner – Runs the struggling island general store. Acts as the primary merchant early in the game and dreams of seeing the marketplace bustling again.

Bookshop Owner – Owns a cozy bookstore filled with travel journals, maps, recipes, and forgotten history. Serves as one of the primary sources of lore and exploration hints.

Blacksmith – Crafts and upgrades the player's tools. As new ores and materials become available, he unlocks increasingly powerful equipment and crafting recipes.

Construction Company Owner – Arrives after the first major restoration milestone. Oversees rebuilding homes, bridges, businesses, and public infrastructure, becoming one of the player's closest partners in restoring Puerto Ventura.

Mayor – Introduced later in the game once the town reaches a healthy population. Organizes festivals, coordinates restoration efforts, and represents Puerto Ventura's return as a thriving community.

Fitness Instructor – Runs beach workouts, hiking clubs, and obstacle courses. Constantly tries to recruit reluctant townspeople into exercising and provides humorous dialogue throughout the game.

Animal Vendor – Sells livestock, pets, and eventually rare tropical animals discovered throughout the island. Expands inventory as additional regions are restored.

Restaurant Owner / Chef – Dreams of turning Puerto Ventura into a world-famous culinary destination using fresh island ingredients. Introduces cooking mechanics, recipes, and food festivals.

Coffee Shop Owner – Opens one of the island's most popular gathering places. NPCs naturally meet here throughout the day, making it an important social hub.

Tavern Owner – Runs the local bar and evening gathering spot. Hosts music nights, celebrations, and plenty of gossip while secretly knowing everyone's business.

Surfer – A laid-back local obsessed with finding the island's perfect wave. Offers surfing challenges, beach activities, and comic relief through his endless optimism.

Plane Crash Survivors – A group of fellow passengers discovered much later on the opposite side of the island. They've somehow survived on coconuts, fish, and increasingly questionable survival strategies before being rescued and integrated into town.

Lost Jungle Explorer – A man who became stranded deep in the jungle years ago and slowly lost touch with civilization. Initially suspicious and eccentric, he eventually rejoins society and becomes an expert jungle guide.

Deep Jungle Tribe – A hidden indigenous community living far beyond the explored jungle. Initially distrustful of outsiders, they become allies after the player earns their trust and provide unique knowledge, crafts, and access to sacred locations.

Island Conspiracy Theorist – An elderly resident who has spent decades documenting strange stories, legends, and unexplained events across Puerto Ventura. Most townspeople dismiss them as eccentric, but many of their outrageous theories turn out to be surprisingly accurate.

Mining Foreman – Oversees the reopening of the island's abandoned mines. Provides mining contracts, excavation projects, and access to increasingly valuable resources.

Desert Couple – Two unconventional free spirits who voluntarily settled in the desert. Inspired by Burning Man and Joshua Tree culture, they practice meditation, build strange art installations, and possess valuable knowledge about restoring the island's ancient irrigation systems.

Volcano Shaman – Guardian of the volcanic region who studies geothermal activity and ancient rituals. Wise but slightly theatrical, accompanied by two loyal assistants whose enthusiasm exceeds their competence.

Volcano Assistants – Apprentices to the Volcano Shaman who constantly argue, misunderstand instructions, and accidentally create comedic situations despite their best intentions.

Witch / Wizard – Lives alone in the island's cursed region and is blamed for nearly every strange occurrence. In reality, they're sarcastic, intelligent, and mostly frustrated that everyone assumes every mystery is their fault.

Influencer – Arrives once Puerto Ventura begins attracting visitors again. Initially interested only in creating content, they gradually become genuinely invested in the island and its community.

Retired Billionaire – Purchases an oceanfront estate after seeking refuge from modern society. Wealthy but surprisingly down-to-earth, they fund ambitious restoration projects simply because they find them interesting.

Ancient City Residents – Five to nine mysterious individuals living within a hidden, highly advanced settlement. Whether they are scientists, descendants of the island's original civilization, or something even more unusual remains one of the game's central mysteries.

Mountain Summit Shamans – A secluded community living high above the clouds in a Shangri-La-like sanctuary. They serve as spiritual guides, offering philosophical insight, advanced abilities, and clues about the island's deepest secrets.

Families & Community

Not every resident begins the game alone. Some characters will have spouses, siblings, parents, children, or lifelong friends, while others will form new families as Puerto Ventura grows. As houses are rebuilt and businesses reopen, new families move to the island, children begin playing in the streets, schools and parks become active, and the town gradually transforms from a nearly abandoned settlement into a vibrant, living community where every new face reflects the island's recovery.

### Planned relationship layers

- Friendship
- Optional romance (later)
- Roles tied to restored businesses (shopkeepers, fishers, caretakers, etc.)

## Arrival timing

Prefer unlocking characters **after** (or as) relevant restorations complete, so people feel like a consequence of healing the island.
