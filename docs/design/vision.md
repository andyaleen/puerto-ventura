# Vision

## Pitch

Puerto Ventura is a cozy 2D top-down island restoration life sim. You inherit a beach cottage on a nearly abandoned paradise and gradually bring districts, businesses, and residents back to life while uncovering why the island fell silent.

## Fantasy

Every session should make the island feel more alive—a restored plaza, a reopened shop, a newly unlocked path, or a returning neighbor.

## Inspiration vs focus

Inspired by Stardew Valley’s satisfying progression loops, but **restoration of the island** is the primary focus—not farm-first expansion.

## Design pillars

- Exploration is rewarding.
- Every gameplay session visibly improves the world.
- Restoration is more important than expansion.
- NPCs have meaningful lives and evolve alongside the town.
- The island itself tells its story.
- Simple systems combine into deep progression.
- The player always has multiple achievable goals.

## Tone

Warm, relaxing, slightly mysterious. Comfortable daily loops with long-term curiosity about the island’s past.
