# Game Design

Summarizes the playable design intent. The longer brief lives in [PRD.md](PRD.md).

## Vision

A cozy 2D top-down island restoration life sim. The player inherits a beach cottage on a nearly abandoned paradise and gradually brings districts, businesses, and residents back to life while uncovering why the island fell silent.

Inspired by Stardew Valley’s satisfying progression, but **restoration of the island** is the primary focus—not farm-first expansion.

## Design pillars

- Exploration is rewarding.
- Every gameplay session visibly improves the world.
- Restoration is more important than expansion.
- NPCs have meaningful lives and evolve alongside the town.
- The island itself tells its story.
- Simple systems combine into deep progression.
- The player always has multiple achievable goals.

## Core loop

```
Explore → Gather → Earn → Upgrade tools → Restore buildings
  → Unlock NPCs → Expand businesses → Reveal history → Unlock regions → Repeat
```

## Systems emphasis

| System | Role |
|--------|------|
| Restoration | Core progression. Projects change maps and unlock content. |
| Fishing | Major pillar (Beach / Harbor). |
| Farming | Exists on farm/home; should not dominate. |
| Mining | Later regions (rocky mining, desert cave). |
| Economy | Resources + money fund restorations and upgrades. |
| NPCs | Return and deepen as districts revive. |
| Story | Environmental clues → late-game mystery regions. |

## Starting areas (intent)

### Beach

Starting shoreline. Lighthouse on the west, central road, dock on the southeast, scattered beach buildings.

- **North exit:** farm / home
- **East exit:** town area

Fishing, beach resources, shipwrecks, cottage feeling.

### Farm / home

Player house and light farming. Separate from the Beach shoreline map.

### Harbor (Beach Harbor)

Central coastal hub. Begins abandoned; evolves with restoration (fountain, docks, cafe, fish shop, etc.).

### City (Idyllic City Center)

Cultural district: museum, town hall, marketplace, gardens, festivals.

### Jungle

Dense tropical forage and vine-blocked paths toward later content.

## Later regions (v1 list)

Volcano, Deep Jungle, Ancient High Tech Town, Desert, Desert Cave, Rocky Mining, Cursed Area, Mountain, Mountain Summit.

These start locked and open through restoration / story gates.

## Player fantasy

Every return to a restored plaza, reopen shop, or newly unlocked path should feel like the island waking up.
